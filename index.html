About

Born Kevin Pearl Sims in San Jose and raised in Menlo Park, Opposite Redd wrote his first rap at around seven or eight years old.

It started with his cousin Buff and two cassette stereos. One played the beat tape while the other recorded, and Buff rapped in between. No studio needed. Watching a full song come together that way hooked him for good.

Growing up in the Bay Area shaped his voice and his curiosity about the process behind the music: the writing, the beats, the recording and turning real life into something people can feel. Today he is an independent artist building his own catalog and the Opposite Redd brand, with street knowledge, confidence and a vision made to last.

You don’t have to wait until everything is perfect to create something powerful. Start with what you have, make it work and keep building.
